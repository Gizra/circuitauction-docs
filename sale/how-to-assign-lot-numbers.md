# How to Assign Lot Numbers

After [items have been added to the sale](../items/how-to-create-an-item.md) you can assign Lot numbers to all the items.

1. Click on `Assign lot numbers to sale items` \(note that this action is not available when the Sale status is set to `New`\).

![image](../assets/screenshots/sale-assign-lot-numbers-button.png)

1. On the Lot numbering page you will see all the items in the sale. You can order the lot numbers in a few ways that can be used in combination.

**Sort by**  
Use the **Sort by** buttons to sort the items, e.g. by **Default** order, **Lot #**, **Temporary lot #**, **Description**, **Category** or **Collection**.

![image](../assets/screenshots/lot-numbering-sort.png)

**Drag and Drop**  
Click on the drag and drop sign \(in the first column\) to move items up and down.

![image](../assets/screenshots/lot-numbering-drag.png)

**Enter Lot number**  
Enter the new Lot number you want to assign the item in the **New lot #** field and click on **Apply**. The item will jump to that position.

![image](../assets/screenshots/lot-numbering-enter-lot.png)

When you change the item's order, you can see in the table the current **Lot #** and the **New lot #** to be assigned.

![image](../assets/screenshots/lot-numbering-current-new.png)

1. Once the items are in order, click on the **Save** button and the new lots will be assigned to the items as Lot number.

![image](../assets/screenshots/lot-numbering-save-lock.png)

1. To avoid changing Lot numbers after a sale is published, you can lock the lot numbering once finished. Click the **Lock lot #** button to lock the sale. If you want to make changes after locking the sale just click the same button again to unlock.
