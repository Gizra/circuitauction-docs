# Reports

Reports are the operational lists that staff print and export. This short video is a guided tour of them.

## What the video covers

1. The tabs list every report, for example sold lots, withdrawn lots and the **Auction Protocol**.
2. **Items sold** is a common starting point. It shows the live report of sold lots for the current sale.
3. **Clients list with address** is a mailing list with addresses that does not depend on a sale.

See also [Sale Context](sale/sale-context.md).
