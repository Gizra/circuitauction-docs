# Consignments

A consignment is the agreement under which a seller places lots with the auction house. This short video is a guided tour of the consignments list and a consignment record.

## What the video covers

1. Open **Consignments** from the main menu.
2. You can filter the consignments by whether the owner has an email address on file.
3. Open a consignment. The consignment number identifies the agreement with the seller.
4. The owner field links to the client who consigned the lots.

See also [Consignment](consignment/README.md), [Consignment Workflow](consignment/consignment-workflow.md), [How to Create a Consignment](consignment/how-to-create-a-consignment.md) and [How to Find an Existing Consignment](consignment/how-to-find-an-existing-consignment.md).
