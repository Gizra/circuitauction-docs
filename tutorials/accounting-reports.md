# Sales by sessions

Sales by sessions holds the accounting reports that finance uses after the sale. This short video is a guided tour of them.

## What the video covers

1. The tabs cover session totals, buyer transactions, seller transactions and sales tax.
2. **Sale sessions** is the default tab. It lists every session of the current sale with its hammer totals.
3. **Refresh** redraws the table. **Recalculate** rebuilds the session totals on the server.
4. **Clients transactions** is the buyer side ledger for the sale.
5. **Consignor transactions** is the same for consignors: what the house owes each seller.
6. **Sales Tax Report** shows the tax report for the current sale.

See also [Sale Context](sale/sale-context.md).
