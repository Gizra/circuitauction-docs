# Bids

The Bids page is the desk for every bid on the current sale. This short video is a guided tour of it.

## What the video covers

1. The sale stats at the top show how many lots are in the sale, even when the Bid Server is offline.
2. When the Bid Server is connected, the table lists every lot and its current bids. Otherwise a warning explains that the server is offline.
3. The status and approval filters narrow the list to sold lots, reserved bids or bids still waiting for approval.
