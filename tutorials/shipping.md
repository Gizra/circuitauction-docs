# Shipping

Seven short videos, one to one and a half minutes each, on shipping an invoice from start to finish. The written guide is in the [Shipping](shipping/README.md) section.

## 1. Creating a shipment

Open the invoice's **Shipping & Packing** tab, click **Add new**, choose the address, click **Get cost and Carrier**, select a rate and save. The billed total adds insurance and handling to the carrier price.

## 3. Packing

Set the shipment to *To pack*, mark each lot as packed, undo with **Mark as Unpacked**, spread the lots over several boxes, and pack by scanning.
