# Categories

Categories are the lot categories used on the public catalogue. This short video is a guided tour of the category tree and its translations.

## What the video covers

1. Open **Categories** under **More** in the main menu.
2. The tree is the live category structure. Drag a row to place it under another category.
3. Type a name in the field to add a category. Nothing is saved until you click **Save**.
4. **Categories translation** lets you edit the category names in every enabled language.
5. Each column is a language. Click a cell to edit the name, or press Escape to cancel.
6. The **Categories** button takes you back to the tree.
