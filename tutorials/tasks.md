# Tasks

Tasks is the to-do list of the auction house, with follow-ups on sales and on clients. This short video is a guided tour of it.

## What the video covers

1. Two tabs split the work. **Sale** tasks belong to the current sale. **Clients** tasks follow a client across sales.
2. The table lists every open task with its title, type, status and the person who last changed it.
3. Filter by title or status in the table footer to find a single task.
4. Switch to the **Clients** tab to see tasks that are not tied to one sale.

See also [Tasks](tasks.md).
