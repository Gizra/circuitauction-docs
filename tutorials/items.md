# Items

Items is where every lot of a sale is managed. This short video is a guided tour of the items list and the item form.

## What the video covers

1. Open **Items** from the main menu.
2. The stat tiles show how many lots are new, ready or already sold.
3. Filter by sold status to focus on lots that still need work, or on lots that are already sold.
4. Open a lot to see its form. It holds the title, the estimates and the opening price shown in the sale.
5. The status control tracks the lot from new, through ready, to sold.

See also [Items](items/README.md), [How to Create an Item](items/how-to-create-an-item.md) and [How to Find an Existing Item](items/how-to-find-an-existing-item.md).
