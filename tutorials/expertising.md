# Expertising

Expertising is how a lot is sent to an outside expert for a certificate. This short video is a guided tour of it.

## What the video covers

1. Expertising is its own invoice type under **Invoices**. Use the tabs to switch between bidder invoices, credit notes and **Expert orders**.
2. The table lists every expertising invoice of the current sale. Open one to edit the request.
3. The request page shows the lot, the certificate label and the chosen expert.
