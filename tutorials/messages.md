# Emails and documents

Emails and documents is the log of the emails the backoffice has sent. This short video is a guided tour of it.

## What the video covers

1. Each row is one sent message, for example an invoice, a statement or a follow-up to a client.
2. Filter by recipient when you need to check that an email went out.
3. The type and content columns show the template that was used and a part of the text.
4. The author column shows which staff member sent it.
