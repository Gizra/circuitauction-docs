# Bidding limit requests

Bidding limit requests are how buyers ask for a higher bidding limit before they bid. This short video is a guided tour of the page.

## What the video covers

1. The page needs a live Bid Server connection. If your token is missing, a warning explains how to refresh it.
2. Requests always belong to one sale, the one chosen in the **Sale context** field at the top.
3. **Pending** holds every request that still needs a decision from the staff.
4. **Approved / Rejected** is the history of the decisions already made.

See also [Sale Context](sale/sale-context.md) and [Login and intro](tutorials/login-and-intro.md).
