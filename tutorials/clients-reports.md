# Clients reports

Clients reports are views of the whole client list that are not tied to one sale. This short video is a guided tour of them.

## What the video covers

1. **Category Bought** is the default report. It shows which categories each client has been buying.
2. The report is a table on the page. You can filter it, sort it, or export it to CSV with the buttons above it.
3. Use it to plan a mailing, a specialist preview or a sale focused on one category.
