# Login page

The login page is where everyone signs in to the backoffice. This short video is a guided tour of the sign-in form and the password reset form.

## What the video covers

1. Enter the username your house issued you, then your password.
2. Click **Login** to sign in. Some houses also show a captcha that you must complete before the button is enabled.
3. **Forgot Password** opens a reset form. Type the email address of your account and click **Submit** to receive a reset link.
4. **Back** returns you to the sign-in form.

See also [Logging into the System](logging-into-the-system.md), [Logging in with an Authenticator App](logging-in-with-an-authenticator-app.md) and [Login and intro](tutorials/login-and-intro.md).
