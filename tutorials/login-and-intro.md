This one-minute video shows how to log in, check the Bid Server connection and choose the sale you work on.

## What the video covers

1. Log in with your username and password. You land on the dashboard, with the main menu on the left.
2. Click the plug icon at the top to see the Bid Server connection. Green means you are connected.
3. If the icon is red, you are not connected. Click **Refresh connection** to fix it.
4. Choose the sale you are working on in the **Sale context** field.
5. Items and Bids now show only the items and bids of that sale. Clients always show the full list.

See also [Logging into the System](logging-into-the-system.md) and [Sale Context](sale/sale-context.md).
