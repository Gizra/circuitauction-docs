# Shipments page

The Shipments page is the packing and dispatch desk for invoices that need to be shipped. This short video is a guided tour of it.

## What the video covers

1. Most of the work happens in the filter bar: pickup date, shipment type, order tags and status.
2. **Filter Today** shows today's pickups. The arrows next to it move the pickup date one day forward or back.
3. The **Type** filter separates parent bundles from regular invoice shipments.
4. **Show orphan ShipStation shipments** lists ShipStation labels that are not attached to any invoice. Turn it on only when you look for such strays. While it is on, the other filters are ignored.
5. **Bulk actions** print invoices and shipping labels for the selected rows.
6. The table lists every matching shipment. Column filters are available for the other fields.
