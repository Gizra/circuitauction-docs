# Bids reports

Bids reports are views of the current sale that come live from the Bid Server. This short video is a guided tour of them.

## What the video covers

1. The tabs list every report.
2. **Bids List** is the starting point. It lists every bid placed in the sale.
3. **Winning bids list** shows only the sold lots of the sale.
4. **Hammer Price list** is the snapshot clerks print as the sale goes on.
5. **Bidder info** groups each bidder number with the lots that bidder is bidding on.
6. **Flagged items** shows the lots that staff have marked for a second look.
