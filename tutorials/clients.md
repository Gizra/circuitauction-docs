# Clients

Clients is where every buyer and seller of the auction house is kept. This short video is a guided tour of the client list and the client record.

## What the video covers

1. **New client** opens a blank client form to register someone new.
2. Filter the list by status to find active clients, or clients who still need follow-up.
3. Open a client to see their record. The main **Client** tab holds the name, the contact details and the fields staff edit most often.
4. Financial and auction settings sit in their own sections on the same form.
5. The **Addresses** tab manages the shipping and billing addresses.
6. The **Bidding info** tab shows the client's bidder numbers and their activity in the current sale.
