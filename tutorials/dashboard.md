# Dashboard

The dashboard is the home screen you see after you sign in. It gives a snapshot of the current sale. This short video is a guided tour of it.

## What the video covers

1. **Live Stats** sit at the top. When an embed URL is configured, the auction-day numbers appear here.
2. **Site traffic** shows how many people visit the public catalogue.
3. The consignments tiles count how many seller agreements are new, in process or finished.
4. The items tiles break the lots down by status: new, ready, sold or returned. Each tile links to a filtered list.
5. The consignor and bidder invoice tiles show how settlement is progressing on both sides of the sale.
6. The clients portlet summarises the client list, for example approved buyers and clients who still need follow-up.
7. **See all items** opens the full items list.

See also [Sale Context](sale/sale-context.md).
