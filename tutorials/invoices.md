# Invoices

Invoices is where winning bids become bills. This short video is a guided tour of the invoices list and a single invoice.

## What the video covers

1. Open **Invoices** from the main menu.
2. The tabs switch between **Bidder invoices**, **Credit notes**, **Return invoices** and **Finder fees orders**.
3. The table lists the invoices of the current sale, with the paid amount and the delivery status.
4. Filter by how much has been paid: nothing, a partial payment or paid in full.
5. Open an invoice. The header and the breadcrumb take you back to the list at any time.

See also [Sale Context](sale/sale-context.md).
