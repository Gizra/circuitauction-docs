# Support tickets

Support tickets are how the auction house reports a bug or a request to Circuit. This short video is a guided tour of the page.

## What the video covers

1. The **Support Tickets** portlet holds everything: the stats, the filters and the list.
2. When the tickets load, a stats strip counts open, in progress, resolved, deployed and closed tickets. If the support service is unavailable, an error is shown instead.
3. **Create Bug report** opens a new ticket.
4. **Refresh** reloads the list. Use it after someone else files a ticket, or when the service is back.
5. The tickets appear in a table you can filter by status and tag. Open a ticket by its number to read the conversation.
