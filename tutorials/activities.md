# Activities

Activities is the log of the work done on records in the backoffice. This short video is a guided tour of it.

## What the video covers

1. Each row is an activity on a record, such as a client, a lot, an invoice or a statement.
2. Filter by record ID when you already know which record you are looking into.
3. The type and info columns tell you what happened and why it was logged.
4. The author column shows who made the change.
