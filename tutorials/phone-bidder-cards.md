# Phone bidder cards

Phone bidder cards are printed by the clerks so that each phone bidder has a paddle for each session. This short video is a guided tour of the page.

## What the video covers

1. Once a sale is selected, the list shows every registered phone bidder, their paddle number and the lots they want.
2. Filter by client name or paddle number to find a single card before you print.
3. The download menu creates a PDF with all phone bidder cards of one session.

See also [How to Download the Phone Bidder Cards List](client/how-to-download-phone-bidder-cards-list.md) and [Sale Context](sale/sale-context.md).
