# Sales

Sales is where every auction is created and managed. This short video is a guided tour of the sales list and the sale page.

## What the video covers

1. **Create New Sale** creates a new sale.
2. Filter the list by status to find upcoming or finished sales.
3. Open a sale to see its details. Each sale has a unique **Sale number** and a status label that shows where it stands in the auction lifecycle.
4. The tabs organise everything about the sale.
5. The **Sale sessions** tab manages the auction sessions of the sale.
6. The **Catalogue Parts** tab groups the lots into the printed catalogue.
7. The **Reports** tab creates the offline protocol and other sale documents.
8. The **Export** menu offers the sale data in several formats.
