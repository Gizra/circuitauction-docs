# Consignment statements

Consignment statements are how the auction house settles with each seller. This short video is a guided tour of the statements list and a single statement.

## What the video covers

1. Open **Consignment statements** from the main menu.
2. Filter by how much of the statement has been paid: nothing, a partial payment or paid in full.
3. Open a statement. The status shows where the statement is in the settlement workflow.
4. The items table lists every sold lot that is paid out on this statement.

See also [How to Find a Consignment Statement](consignment/how-to-find-consignment-statement.md), [How Consignment Statements are Created](consignment/how-consignment-statement-are-created.md) and [How to Find a Related Consignment Statement](consignment/how-to-find-related-consignment-statement.md).
